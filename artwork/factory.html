Factory

Date 2026
Medium Ink & Watercolour on Paper
Dimensions 1140mm x 600mm
Series Traces of Place

Conceived through first-hand experience of industrial workplaces, "Factory" observes how workers humanise systems designed for efficiency through routine and repetition.

The drawing is less concerned with production itself than with the moments that interrupt it: clock in, talk, task, flirt, task, eat, task, smoke break, task, lunch, task, check phone, task, clean, task, clock out.
