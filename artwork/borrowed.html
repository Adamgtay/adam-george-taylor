Borrowed

Date 2026
Medium Ink
Dimensions 1310mm x 500mm
Series Imprints

A pen and ink illustration exploring the traces of use, ownership and human connection carried by everyday tools. Passed on by friends and family, each tool bears the marks of previous hands, recording a history of use that extends beyond its practical purpose. By observing and drawing these objects, I add my own layer of use to their histories. The act of recording becomes another way of handling them, selecting and preserving the scratches, worn surfaces and small details that distinguish each tool. What interests me is how ordinary objects carry traces of the people who have used them, and how the simple act of passing something on can embody a lasting connection between people.
