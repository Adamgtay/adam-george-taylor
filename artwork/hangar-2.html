Hangar 2

Date 2026
Medium Ink & Watercolour on Paper
Dimensions 2340mm x 530mm
Series Traces of Place

A panoramic drawing of a museum hangar.

The composition presents a cross-section of visitors, aircraft, artefacts and mannequins distributed throughout the gallery spaces.

Observing everything from the quiet diligence of volunteer restoration to the unsettling display of wartime memorabilia, the drawing unfolds the uneasy coexistence of preservation, nostalgia and the enduring presence of conflict.
