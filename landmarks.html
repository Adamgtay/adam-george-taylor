Landmarks

Landmarks brings together collaged observational drawings developed through an interest in the physical character and visual identity of places. Beginning with on-site sketches, photographs and written observations, the work explores the structures, features and incidental details that define our experience of particular environments. Rather than reworking these observations into a unified drawing, the original sketches are retained and assembled into compositions through collage. Different viewpoints, fragments and individual observations coexist within the finished image, preserving something of the immediacy and variety of the initial encounters. The process allows the character of a place to emerge through the accumulation and arrangement of these recorded details. The resulting works consider how we recognise, experience and construct an understanding of our surroundings, finding significance in both prominent features and otherwise overlooked aspects of the built and natural environment.
