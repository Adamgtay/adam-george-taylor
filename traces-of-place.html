Field Notes: Traces of Place

Field Notes: Traces of Place brings together observational drawings and visual investigations developed through an interest in the character of places and the everyday activities that take place within them. Beginning with on-site sketches, photographs and written observations, the work explores how environments are shaped by the people who inhabit, use and pass through them. Architecture, objects, gestures and everyday routines become points of interest, revealing the often unnoticed relationships between people and their surroundings. Rather than setting out to illustrate a particular argument, the process begins with looking and recording. Through the subsequent examination and arrangement of these observations in the studio, connections, contrasts and unexpected meanings gradually emerge. The resulting drawings reconstruct fragments of lived experience, offering considered perspectives on the places we occupy and the lives unfolding within them.
